One liquidity layer

Built around fragmented liquidity.

Liquidity is distributed across exchanges, chains and pools. LQC is designed around a unified infrastructure model that can route, allocate and reuse liquidity across multiple decentralized markets.

01 / MULTI-DEX

Liquidity connectivity

A framework for connecting multiple DEX liquidity venues instead of depending on a single exchange or pool.

02 / ROUTING

Optimal execution paths

Routing logic is intended to evaluate available liquidity paths and improve capital and execution efficiency across venues.

03 / CROSS-CHAIN

Capital across networks

The protocol scope includes cross-chain liquidity movement so liquidity can become more useful beyond isolated ecosystems.

Protocol model

Liquidity can do more than sit in a pool.

The LQC architecture combines liquidity provision, route selection, lending utility and cross-chain movement within one broader liquidity framework.

01 — ConnectAggregate access to liquidity across multiple DEX ecosystems.
02 — RouteIdentify efficient routes based on available liquidity conditions.
03 — UtilizeExtend liquidity utility into lending, staking and related capital functions.
04 — RecycleUse LQC in designated lending and repayment fee flows with a burn mechanism.
