One liquidity layer

Fragmented liquidity becomes usable liquidity.

Liquidity is distributed across DEXs, pools, networks and bridges. LQC is designed as an infrastructure layer that connects those venues and makes routing, allocation and capital reuse more coherent.

01 / MULTI-DEX

Connect liquidity venues

Aggregate access across many decentralized liquidity venues rather than relying on one exchange or one pool.

02 / ROUTING

Compare multiple paths

Evaluate candidate paths across available venues and identify a route intended to improve execution and capital efficiency.

03 / CROSS-CHAIN

Move capital across networks

Extend liquidity utility beyond isolated chains through cross-chain capital movement and bridge-aware routing.

Perpetual trading · Planned module

Continuous markets, controlled risk.

The planned LQC perpetual module is designed to connect long and short positions with oracle pricing, recurring funding, margin controls and layered liquidation protection.

Phase 4 · Planned module

A market loop that never expires.

Positions remain open while margin requirements are met. Funding helps align the perpetual market with its reference price, while insurance and ADL are designed as layered safeguards.

01Open a long or short position against available market liquidity.
02Oracle and mark-price controls continuously update position risk.
03Funding transfers balance market bias between long and short positions.
04Margin, liquidation, insurance and ADL form a layered risk system.
Activation remains subject to testnet validation, independent security audit, sufficient market depth and applicable legal review.
