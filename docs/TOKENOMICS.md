## Control and Disclosure Principles

- The fixed-supply token contract must not expose unrestricted minting.
- Allocation and vesting contracts must be published and source-verified before production use.
- Treasury and allocation administration should use disclosed multisig and timelock controls.
- Material allocation or release changes require governance approval and an updated public disclosure.
- Contract addresses, holder wallets, deployment transactions, and explorer verification will be published after reviewed deployment.
- Strategic-investor proposals remain separate, non-binding drafts until formally approved; they do not amend this allocation table.

The machine-readable source for this disclosure is [`tokenomics.json`](tokenomics.json).
