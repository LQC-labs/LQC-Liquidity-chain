# Master 6/14 — Supply Earn Reuse Gate

Status: **reuse / verification gate**

Under the agreed 15-stage reuse-first plan, Supply Earn is not rebuilt as a separate yield engine because the current Lending stack already contains the required accounting foundation:

- borrow rate is utilization-dependent;
- supply rate is derived from borrower interest and utilization;
- reserve factor separates protocol reserves from supplier yield;
- supplier claims are represented by indexed liquidity shares;
- accrued supplier yield is reflected through the supply index;
- existing Lending E2E already verifies borrower interest, supplier yield and protocol reserves.

## Product boundary

Supply Earn means the user supplies the Lending debt asset and receives variable yield generated by actual borrowing activity.

It is **not**:
- a fixed or guaranteed APY;
- an LQC staking program;
- a Treasury subsidy;
- a hidden mint path;
- a mechanism for masking bad debt.

The existing Lending Core accounting is reused. No duplicate Earn vault is introduced.

## Remaining gap

The remaining Supply Earn gap is primarily presentation/integration:
- expose supplied balance, variable supply rate and accrued earnings to the product/UI layer;
- carry the same accounting into Lending E2E and Full BSC Integration evidence;
- preserve pause-safe withdrawals and bad-debt/reserve rules.

## Exit meaning

Passing 6/14 means:

**SUPPLY EARN ACCOUNTING FOUNDATION REUSED AND VERIFIED**

It does not authorize any fixed APY claim, token-emission reward or market activation.

Next: **6/15 LQC Staking Vault**, which is a genuine implementation gap and must remain accounting-isolated from Lending Core.
